Spouses, registered partners and children can be insured through a member of a statutory health
insurer without paying their own contributions. This is called family insurance
(Familienversicherung). It is only possible if their own income stays below a limit and other
conditions apply. Insurers check these conditions regularly and send a questionnaire. If the
questionnaire is not returned, the insurer can end the family insurance. The person then needs their
own insurance.
