A landlord's notice of termination must be in writing and must state reasons. A tenant can object to
an ordinary termination if moving out would be an unacceptable hardship. The objection must be in
writing and must usually reach the landlord at least two months before the tenancy ends. Whether a
termination is valid depends on the individual case. Tenants should contact a tenant association, a
counselling service or a lawyer immediately.
