In Germany, one broadcasting fee is due for each home, no matter how many people live there or which
devices they use. The Beitragsservice collects it. People who receive certain social benefits can
apply for an exemption, for example basic income support (Grundsicherungsgeld, formerly Bürgergeld),
social assistance, or BAföG when they do not live with their parents. The exemption must be applied
for; it does not start automatically. Unpaid fees can lead to reminders, additional charges and
enforcement.
