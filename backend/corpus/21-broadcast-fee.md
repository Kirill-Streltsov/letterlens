In Germany, one broadcasting fee is due for each home, no matter how many people live there or which
devices they use. The Beitragsservice collects it. People who receive certain social benefits, for
example Bürgergeld, basic income support or BAföG, can apply for an exemption. The exemption must be
applied for; it does not start automatically. Unpaid fees can lead to reminders, additional charges
and enforcement.
