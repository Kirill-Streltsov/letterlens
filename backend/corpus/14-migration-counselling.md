Migration counselling for adults (Migrationsberatung für erwachsene Zuwanderer, MBE) helps
immigrants over 27 years of age. Youth migration services (Jugendmigrationsdienste, JMD) help people
from 12 to 27 years. Both services are free and confidential, are funded by the federal government
and are run by welfare organisations in many cities. Counsellors explain letters, help with
applications and refer to specialist services. Many offices offer counselling in several languages.
