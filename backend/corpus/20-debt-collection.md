A debt collection agency collects a claim for another company. The letter must name the creditor,
the reason for the claim and a breakdown of the amounts, including interest and costs. Collection
agencies must be registered in the public legal services register (Rechtsdienstleistungsregister).
Check whether the claim exists and whether the amounts are correct. If a claim is not justified, say
so in writing. Consumer advice centres (Verbraucherzentralen) and debt counselling services help
with such letters.
