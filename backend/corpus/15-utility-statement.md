Tenants usually pay monthly advance payments for operating costs such as heating, water and waste.
Once a year the landlord sends a statement. It must arrive within twelve months after the end of the
billing period; after that, the landlord can usually not ask for an additional payment. Tenants can
raise objections until the end of the twelfth month after they received the statement. Tenants have
the right to see the receipts (Belegeinsicht). Tenant associations (Mietervereine) check statements
for their members.
