Write down the date in the letter and keep the envelope. Act early, because postal delivery takes
time. If you need more time, ask in writing before the date ends; for formal remedies like
Widerspruch or Einspruch, the period can usually not be extended. Send important documents in a way
that you can prove later, for example by fax with a transmission report, by registered mail with
return receipt, or by delivery in person with a receipt stamp. Keep copies of everything you send.
