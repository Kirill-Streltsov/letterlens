A landlord can ask the tenant to agree to a higher rent up to the local comparative rent
(ortsübliche Vergleichsmiete), often with reference to a rent index (Mietspiegel). The tenant has
time to think until the end of the second calendar month after receiving the request. If the tenant
agrees, the new rent applies from the start of the third calendar month after receipt. The rent may
not rise by more than 20 percent within three years; in some cities the limit is 15 percent. If the
tenant does not agree, the landlord can go to court. Tenant associations can check whether the
request is correct.
