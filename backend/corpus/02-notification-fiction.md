Many periods start when an official decision counts as received ("Bekanntgabe"). For decisions sent
by ordinary post after 31 December 2024, the law assumes receipt on the fourth day after posting
(before 2025 it was the third day). The date of posting is usually the date on the letter. If the
fourth day is a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, courts often move it to the next working day. If
the letter really arrived later, the authority must prove the date of receipt in case of doubt. Keep
the envelope, because it can show when the letter arrived.
