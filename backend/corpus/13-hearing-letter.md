Before an authority makes a decision that affects a person's rights, it must usually give that
person the opportunity to comment. This letter is called Anhörung. It describes what the authority
plans to do and why, and names a date for a reply. A person can send a written statement and missing
documents. An Anhörung is not yet the final decision, but the decision that follows can have serious
effects. Many people get help from a counselling service before they reply.
