An Einspruch is the formal way to ask the tax office (Finanzamt) or the Familienkasse to review a
decision. The section "Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung" at the end of the letter names the period. Usually it
is one month after the letter counts as received. The objection can be sent in writing or
electronically, for example through ELSTER. It is free of charge. An objection does not stop a
payment that is due. To pause the payment while the objection is open, a person must ask separately
for "Aussetzung der Vollziehung" (§ 361 AO). Decisions about child benefit (Kindergeld) from the
Familienkasse follow tax law, so the remedy there is also called Einspruch.
