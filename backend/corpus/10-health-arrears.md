People who pay their own health insurance contributions, for example voluntary members, must pay
them every month. If arrears reach two months of contributions and remain unpaid after a reminder,
the insurer can suspend most benefits. Treatment of acute illness and pain, and care during
pregnancy and maternity, stay available. Late contributions also cost a surcharge of one percent per
started month. Insurers can agree to payment in instalments. People with low income can ask the
Jobcenter or the social welfare office whether contributions can be covered.
