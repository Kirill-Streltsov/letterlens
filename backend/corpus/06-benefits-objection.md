Decisions of the Jobcenter, the employment agency (Agentur für Arbeit), the social welfare office
and the health or care insurers can be challenged with a Widerspruch. The period is one month after
the decision counts as received. The Widerspruch must be sent in writing or declared at the office,
where staff write it down ("zur Niederschrift"). The procedure is free of charge. The office then
checks the decision again and sends a new decision (Widerspruchsbescheid). Counselling services and
lawyers can help. People with low income can get legal advice through Beratungshilfe.
