Some official letters are delivered "gegen Empfangsbekenntnis". The authority then asks the
recipient to sign a short form, the acknowledgement of receipt, with the date on which the letter
was handed over or received. Usually the form is attached to the letter, and the recipient sends it
back. Signing it confirms only that the letter arrived. It does not mean that the recipient agrees
with the content. The date on the form can matter, because periods in the letter can start on the
day of delivery. Keep a copy of the signed form.
