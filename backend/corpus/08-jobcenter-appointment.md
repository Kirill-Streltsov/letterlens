Since 1 July 2026 the basic income support for job seekers is called Grundsicherungsgeld; it
replaced Bürgergeld, and some letters still use the old name until the end of 2026. The Jobcenter
can invite people who receive it to an appointment, or to a medical or psychological examination. If
a person repeatedly does not come after a written notice of the consequences, and has no important
reason ("wichtiger Grund"), the Jobcenter can reduce the payment by 30 percent of the standard rate
for one month each time. The person must explain and prove the important reason, for example illness
with a medical certificate. If you cannot come, tell the Jobcenter before the appointment and ask
for a new date.
