People who apply for or receive social benefits must state all facts that matter for the benefit and
provide evidence when asked. If they do not, the office can refuse or withdraw the benefit until
they cooperate. Before it does this, the office must send a written warning and give a reasonable
period. If the documents cannot be found in time, it helps to tell the office in writing and to ask
for more time. Keep copies of everything that you send, and ask for a confirmation of receipt.
