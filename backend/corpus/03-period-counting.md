A period that starts with an event, such as receipt of a letter, starts on the day after that event.
A period of one month ends on the day of the next month that has the same number as the start day.
If that day does not exist, for example 31 February, it ends on the last day of the month. A period
of two weeks ends on the same weekday two weeks later. If the last day is a Saturday, Sunday or
public holiday at the place where the act must be done, the period ends on the next working day.
Public holidays differ between the federal states.
