If a person must file a tax return and the tax office has not received it, the office sends a
reminder with a date. If the return is still late, the office can charge a late filing surcharge
(Verspätungszuschlag). Tax returns can be filed for free through ELSTER, the online portal of the
tax administration. Employees can get help from a wage tax assistance association
(Lohnsteuerhilfeverein) for a membership fee, or from a tax adviser. If more time is needed, a
person can ask the tax office for an extension before the date.
