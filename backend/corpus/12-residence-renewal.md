A residence permit must be renewed before it expires. If a person applies for renewal in time, the
previous permit counts as continuing until the immigration office (Ausländerbehörde) decides. The
office then issues a certificate called Fiktionsbescheinigung. A late application can cause a gap,
which can affect work, benefits and later permits. The office usually asks for a passport, a
biometric photo, proof of income, a rental contract and proof of health insurance. Fees are set in
the Residence Ordinance (Aufenthaltsverordnung).
