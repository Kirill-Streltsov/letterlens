Unpaid rent can have serious consequences. If a tenant is behind with rent above certain limits, the
landlord can end the tenancy without notice. In some cases the tenant can make such a termination
invalid by paying all arrears within two months after an eviction lawsuit has been served. The
Jobcenter or the social welfare office can sometimes take over rent debts as a loan to keep the home
(§ 22 (8) SGB II, for people who receive
Grundsicherungsgeld). Contact a counselling service or a tenant association early.
