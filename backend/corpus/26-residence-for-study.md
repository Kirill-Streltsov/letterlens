A residence permit for study is usually granted or renewed for two years. It is renewed if the
purpose of the stay, the degree, is not yet reached and can still be reached within a reasonable
period. To judge this, the immigration office can involve the university, and it looks at the study
progress, for example semesters and credit points. The permit allows employment of up to 140 working
days per year; student jobs at the university do not count, and days with up to four hours of work
can count as half days. The main purpose of the stay must remain the study. In practice, an office
can first send a formal warning before it refuses a renewal. Migration counselling services and the
international office of the university can help to plan the remaining studies.
