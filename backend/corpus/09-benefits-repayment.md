An office can cancel or change a decision about benefits, for example when income or the household
changed. Benefits paid without a legal basis must then be paid back. The repayment decision can be
challenged like other decisions; the remedy and the period are in the "Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung". A
person who cannot pay the full amount at once can ask for payment in instalments. The office may
also offset the debt against current benefits within legal limits.
