A hearing form (Anhörungsbogen) comes before a fine notice. It asks for personal details, which a person
must give correctly. A person does not have to comment on the accusation itself. A warning fine
(Verwarnungsgeld) is only valid if the person agrees and pays it, usually within one week. A fine notice
(Bußgeldbescheid) sets a fine, fees and sometimes a driving ban (Fahrverbot) or points. An objection
(Einspruch) against a fine notice is possible within two weeks after delivery (Zustellung), in writing or
recorded at the authority that issued it. The delivery date is written on the yellow envelope. Without an
objection, the notice becomes final and the fine must be paid. A driving ban often starts when the driving
licence is handed in; in some cases the person can choose a start within four months after the notice is final.
