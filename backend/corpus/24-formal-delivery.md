Some letters are delivered formally with a delivery certificate. They come in a yellow envelope. The
postal worker writes the date of delivery on the envelope. If nobody is at home, the letter can be
put in the letterbox or deposited at a post office, and it still counts as delivered on that day.
Periods then start from the delivery date. Keep the envelope, because it shows this date.
