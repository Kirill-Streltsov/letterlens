A Mahnbescheid comes from a court (Mahngericht), usually in a yellow envelope. It is different from
a normal reminder from a company. The court does not check whether the claim is correct. The person
can object (Widerspruch) within two weeks after delivery, with the form that comes with the letter.
If the person does nothing, the creditor can ask for an enforcement order (Vollstreckungsbescheid).
Against that order, an objection (Einspruch) is possible within two weeks. After that, the creditor
can enforce the claim.
