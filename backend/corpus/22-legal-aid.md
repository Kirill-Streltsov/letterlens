People with low income can get legal advice from a lawyer at a very small cost. They apply at the
local court (Amtsgericht) for a certificate (Beratungsschein). The court checks income and costs.
With the certificate, a lawyer advises the person and, if needed, writes to the authority. Some
lawyers also help with the application. Social and migration counselling services can explain the
procedure.
