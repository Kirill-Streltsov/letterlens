If a tax is not paid by the due date, the tax office charges a late payment surcharge. It is one
percent of the unpaid tax, rounded down to the next 50 euros, for each month or part of a month of
delay. A person who cannot pay on time can ask the tax office in writing for a deferral (Stundung)
or for payment in instalments. The tax office decides on such requests. It is usually better to
contact the office before the due date than after it.
